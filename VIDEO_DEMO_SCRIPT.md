# Video Demo Script: SAT/ACT Adaptive Tutoring
**Duration:** 2.5–3 minutes  
**Format:** Screen recording (no voice) + on-screen subtitles + background music  
**Music:** Suggest: Ambient/calm (YouTube Audio Library: "Ambient Strings", "Minimal Piano", or "Peaceful Strings")

---

## Recording Setup

**Software Options:**
- **Mac:** QuickTime Player (built-in) + add music/subtitles in iMovie or CapCut after
- **Windows:** OBS Studio (free) or Camtasia (paid)
- **All platforms:** CapCut (free, built-in subtitle + music tools)

**Screen settings:** 1920×1080 @ 60fps recommended

---

## Timing Tips

- **Don't rush:** Viewers need 1–2 seconds to read each scene
- **Pause on key screens:** Let the clearing image breathe for 2 sec before clicking next
- **Smooth mouse movements:** Fast clicks look janky; slow moves look intentional
- **Wait for load screens:** Let transitions complete before narrating (captions stay on screen)

Good luck! If you hit any snags with CapCut, let me know.
