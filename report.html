Pitch: An adaptive tutoring app that personalizes SAT/ACT prep by analyzing student errors, assigning targeted practice, and rewarding progress with gamified gems.

1. Motivation: The Problem

For Students: After taking a practice test, a student sees 800+ questions from dozens of topics. Which should they drill? How many times? When do they level up? Without personalization, they waste hours on material they already know and burn out on unfamiliar content.

For Teachers: Assigning homework to 20 students means guessing which topics are weak for each one. Creating individual assignments takes 6+ hours per week. Teachers resort to one-size-fits-all curricula or hiring tutors at $50–100/hr—costs families can't always afford.

The Cost: Inefficient studying means students need 100+ hours to reach their target instead of 60–80. For test prep, that's wasted time and money; 40% of students quit prep altogether.

2. What Is New Here

Existing apps (Khan Academy, Bluebook, Kaplan) offer questions or lessons, but not personalization. ChatGPT is flexible but has no curriculum structure or progress tracking. This project combines three novel ideas:

Error-Driven Learning
Gem Progression
Teacher Tools
  • AI Personalization: When a student answers incorrectly, an AI agent classifies the error (e.g., "misconception: discriminant formula") and the teacher assigns practice that fixes it specifically.
  • Gem System: Students earn color gems (gold/silver/bronze/purple) for 10 correct at each tier (Easy/Medium/Hard/Challenge). Progress shows "5/10 toward next gem" to encourage completion.
  • Teacher Dashboard: Teachers assign error-specific homework in seconds, download assignments by student, and see mistakes across their class at a glance.
  • Immersive Design: Consistent forest theming (Times New Roman, green palette, clearing maps) makes studying feel like a quest, not a worksheet.

3. The Agents

Agent 1: Error Analyzer

Reads a wrong answer and classifies the error (misconception, misread, calculation). Calls Claude 3.5 Sonnet with structured prompts to avoid injection attacks.

Agent 2: Assignment Planner

When a teacher wants to assign homework, this agent fetches the student's errors, queries the bank for fresh questions at the right tier, and creates the assignment—all in 30 seconds.

Agent 3: Gem Tracker

Increments a counter when a student answers correctly. At 10 correct, a gem unlocks. Deterministic; no AI needed. Stored locally in localStorage.

Result: Classified as "misread", not executed. ✓

Test 2: Cross-Student Access

Tried to view another student's errors via localStorage edit.
Result: UI isolates by student ID; Supabase RLS blocks queries. ✓

Test 3: Assignment Duplicates

Assigned homework to student who completed 5 Factoring questions.
Result: Query excluded those 5; returned 8 fresh questions. ✓
