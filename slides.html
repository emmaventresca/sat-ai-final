How the Agents Work

1. Error Analyzer

Student answers wrong → AI classifies HOW (misconception, misread, calculation)

2. Assignment Planner

Teacher clicks "error practice" → finds 5 fresh questions at right tier in 30s

3. Gem Tracker

10 correct at Easy → gold gem. 10 at Medium → silver. Shows "5/10 toward next gem"
