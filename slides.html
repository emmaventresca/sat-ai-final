Student Experience

💎 Gem Progression System

10 correct at Easy → Gold gem

10 correct at Medium → Silver gem

10 correct at Hard → Bronze gem

10 correct at Challenge → Amethyst gem

Shows "5/10 toward next gem" for clear milestones

🔴 Error Log

Every wrong answer is tracked, categorized by misconception

Students see trends: "You get this wrong in word problems"

Fix an error once → it leaves the list (gamified feedback)

Teacher Backend

👥 Student Roster

Teacher generates codes → students register with codes

Roster shows all students + score summaries (SAT/ACT, Reading, Math)

Row-level security: students can't see others' data

📋 Assignment Creation

Targeted picking: Choose exam → domain → skill → tier

Error-driven: Click "Assign mistakes" → auto-pulls 10 wrong questions from a student

Fresh questions: Tool auto-generates variants at the right difficulty

📊 Progress Tracking

See per-student: gems earned, errors remaining, time invested

Export assignments by student to folders

Download lesson plans as PDF or worksheet
